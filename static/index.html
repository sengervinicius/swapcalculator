Formula (USD → BRL)
The hedge cost/benefit is calculated as the difference between the implied USD rate in Brazil and the actual USD rate:
Hedge Benefit (bps) = (Cupom Cambial − SOFR) × 100
Cupom Cambial = Implied USD rate in Brazil (from B3 DDI futures)
SOFR = Secured Overnight Financing Rate (actual USD rate)
A positive value means you benefit from hedging. A negative value would mean a hedging cost.
Why Cupom Cambial?
The Cupom Cambial already incorporates the convertibility premium — the cost of USD being "trapped" in Brazil's financial system vs. freely tradeable offshore USD:
Theoretical max gain = CDI − SOFR ≈ 900 bps
Actual hedge gain = Cupom Cambial − SOFR ≈ 50-150 bps
Difference = Convertibility Premium (already priced in by market)
Limitations
⚠️ This calculation represents the expected hedge cost/benefit based on current market rates. It does not include: (1) Transaction costs (bid-offer spreads on futures), (2) Slippage from discrete hedging, (3) Roll costs for multi-year hedges. Use manual override for institution-specific adjustments.
Based on CIP (Covered Interest Parity) principles. Reference: Patry & Mozley (2021) "Future Hedging Costs"
